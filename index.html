Panthera tigris

Bengal Tiger

Conservation Status
Endangered
Screen Appearances
142 documented
Primary Habitat
South & Southeast Asian forests
Wild Population
~4,500 individuals

The tiger is one of cinema's most iconic animals - a symbol of power, mystery, and wilderness. From Life of Pi to The Jungle Book, tigers have captivated audiences for over a century. Yet their screen ubiquity masks a precarious reality: fewer than 4,500 remain in the wild.

Each film appearance is an opportunity - a moment when millions of viewers connect with an animal whose habitat shrinks by the day.
