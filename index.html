Every Species Has a Story

Every story shapes our response.

The Eco-Cinema Observatory connects the cultural record of how species appear on our screens with the ecological record of how they fare in the wild. By mapping these connections, we begin to understand how human attention relates to species fate.

Eco-Cinema Observatory - An open exploration project

This page presents an interactive 3D cinematic experience exploring the relationship between species in film and conservation. The experience includes a rotating globe with data visualization columns, a species gallery with ten flagship species, and a closing vision section. Use the scroll to navigate through sections, or access individual species pages via the links below.
