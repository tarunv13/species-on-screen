Methodology

Rose's Visual Methodology

Following Gillian Rose's critical visual methodology framework (2016), this observatory examines species representation across three sites of meaning: the site of production (how filmmakers construct animal narratives), the site of the image itself (what visual techniques are employed), and the site of audiencing (how viewers interpret and respond to these representations).

COM-B Behavioural Model

The COM-B model (Michie et al., 2011) provides the behavioural science framework for understanding the gap between media exposure and conservation action. We examine how cinematic representation affects Capability (knowledge and skills), Opportunity (social and physical enablers), and Motivation (reflective and automatic drivers) for pro-conservation behaviour.

Every Species Has a Story

Every story shapes our response.

The Eco-Cinema Observatory connects the cultural record of how species appear on our screens with the ecological record of how they fare in the wild. By mapping these connections, we begin to understand how human attention relates to species fate.
