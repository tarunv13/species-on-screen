The V1.2b → V1.3 → V1.4 arc was authored between 2026-07-25 and 2026-08-12 and
then **sat uncommitted in the working tree for four weeks**. Three commits from
that arc had landed (`e36b965` the ADR, `5c040af` the plate slot + starfield,

found the tree green (`npm run verify`, 17 checks, exit 0), split the work into

That gap is itself the finding worth keeping: **green and complete is not the

was true of `main` and false of the tree. The four-week exposure was to a disk,

## The arc

### 1 · The visual layer was recovered, then actually installed (`848b5e4`, `eda6869`)

The 2026-07-25 ADR (`.agents/decisions/2026-07-25-visual-layer-recovery.md`)
established that §9's no-image clause was dead and that the asset slots had been
empty since 2026-06-22 — with "none yet" provenance that read as an editorial
decision to withhold rather than as a gap. This arc is the first work to put
real assets through those slots.

**The globe** (`848b5e4`). The planetary view was textured from an unpkg CDN
JPEG with a silent colour fallback — a third-party network dependency on the
first thing anyone sees. Retired for local public-domain NASA imagery: Blue
Marble Next Generation w/ Topography & Bathymetry (August 2004, record 73776)
for day, Earth at Night 2012 "Black Marble" (record 79765) for night, blended
across the terminator by a custom shader, 708 KB combined against a 4 MB budget.

August is chosen for **seasonality, not convenience**: cloud-free by
construction, maximal northern vegetation, minimal Arctic ice, monsoon-green
Sundarbans. The sun is fixed far upper-left in world space so the camera-facing
hemisphere reads mostly **night** with a thin day crescent. Held in darkness was
already free and already better; the texture is not brightened to show it off.

Two things carried in the same slice. `prefers-reduced-motion` finally has a
real path through the landing sequence — the camera is placed at its final
framing directly and captions settle after a beat, where previously the 6s
approach ran regardless. And `mediaCounts` was deleted: written on every dossier
load, never read.

**The light** (`eda6869`). See below — it earns its own section.

### 2 · The light direction, and a retired constraint

`src/places/lamp-pool.js` transcribes "The Light of the Observation" from the
design artifact's three-state reference render. Darkness is the **surround**;
the subject is lit with the light the record was made under. The flat 0.45 veil
over the scene plate is retired — it dimmed the whole frame evenly, which is not
how a record made under a lamp looks.

Draw order is the reference's, verbatim: plate → grade → drifting lamp centre →
caustics + beam scatter → lamp-pool vignette → in-beam marine snow. The lamp
drifts on incommensurable periods (~45–65 s) so the frame never visibly repeats.

Two recipes, because the two records were made under different light. `crossing`
is surface water: cool grade, wide lamp (0.49), caustics, beam scatter.
`epr-vents` is 2550 m: a **neutral desaturation** grade rather than a cool cast
(no water column at that depth to shift colour; the Alvin lamp is broadband and
close), a narrow hard lamp (0.30), heavy snow, and **no caustics** — there is no
surface light down there to make them. The vent glow stays warm and is drawn
over the pool: it is thermal and mineral, not lamplight.

**The 0.62 equal-mean-alpha constraint is retired.** It asked the lamp pool to
integrate to the same mean coverage as the flat veil it replaced. It cannot, and
should not — a redistribution of darkness is the whole point, so the surround
goes darker than a flat veil and a small pool goes brighter (~0.516 at 16:9 by
design). Holding the number would have forced the light back toward the veil it
was meant to replace.

The reference render is authoritative, not a mean-coverage number. Article VI is
"nothing shouts" — a **peak and edge** property, not a mean — so the gate became
two bounds, evaluated **at rest** (static mean lamp centre):

    corner alpha >= 0.94     nothing shouts at the edges
    centre alpha <= 0.10     the plate genuinely reads as lit

Both lamp widths verified at the static centre: corner ≥ 0.95, centre 0.05. The
dip toward ~0.88 as the pool drifts corner-ward is the effect working, not a
violation — the bound is at-rest. Portrait 9:16 (0.936) is not a target aspect.
The area-weighted-mean integrator that surfaced the original discrepancy is kept
as `_meanAlpha`, a dev utility, no longer gating.

This is a constraint replaced by a **better-specified** constraint, not a
constraint abandoned. That distinction is the reason it is recorded here.

### 3 · The coral plate — CLOSED as a documented failure

The `crossing` scene plate slot is **empty, and stays empty.** This is a closed
failure, not an open task, and it should not be reopened.

What was tried, and what it produced:

- **Route 1 — NOAA.** Ocean Exploration, the NOAA Photo Library, and Coral Reef
  Watch. NOAA reef imagery is Micronesia and the Pacific US; none of it is
  Coral-Triangle-proper.
- **Route 2 — Wikimedia Commons.** Yields divers, single-species close-ups,
  aerial scenics, or generics with unstated locality.

Neither route produced a compliant asset. The contract a replacement must meet
is recorded in `public/art/STYLE-GUIDE.md`: landscape (3:2–2:1), reading as a
reef slope, wall or structure you are **inside** rather than a subject you are
looking at, Coral-Triangle-proper with **stated locality**, ≥1400px, no diver,
CC0/CC-BY/CC-BY-SA.

The candidate that was found — Nick Hobgood's *Dendronephthya* soft coral,
Komodo, CC-BY-SA-3.0 — was **vetoed on 2026-07-28** and remains vetoed: it is
portrait (1260×1680, so cover-fitting into 16:9 discards ~58% of the frame) and
it is a single soft-coral *colony* — a subject, not a place.

**Locality honesty was not loosened to fill the slot.** That is the decision.
An empty slot that is legible as a defect is better than a filled slot that
quietly misstates where a record was made.

**This session held the vetoed placeholder out of the commit.** `epr-vents.webp`
landed (USGS public domain, East Pacific Rise 9°39′N at 2550 m, DSV *Alvin*
1991, Pat Shanks) because the deep-vent recipe is built for that specific
record. `public/art/scene/crossing.webp` was **not** committed, on three
grounds: the STYLE-GUIDE's own rule that an uninstalled design must stay legible
as uninstalled; the fact that committing a vetoed placeholder installs a
non-design onto the doctrine-governed cinematic surface; and an unresolved
attribution problem — CC-BY-SA-3.0 requires visible attribution, and the
cinematic surface forbids chrome (D3). That third point is a genuine open
question for any ShareAlike asset on that surface, and is flagged rather than
answered here.

Consequence, stated plainly: **the `crossing` lamp-pool recipe is dormant in
production.** With no plate, `paintBase` renders the procedural scene unchanged
(its `veil` parameter defaults to 1 — byte-identical to pre-plate behaviour).
The surface-water recipe ships as unexercised code.

### 5 · Third-party edit hooks — refused, and the refusal corrected itself (`e5af453`)

`pbakaus/impeccable` was installed during tooling setup and ships three edit
hooks. The ruling (`.agents/decisions/2026-08-12-third-party-edit-hooks.md`):
**off by default**, kept as a read-and-advise tool, with a scoped trial
permitted on `atlas/` and `notes/` alone and **never** on `src/places/` or
`index.html`.

The ADR's value is that it **retracts its own first argument.** That argument
claimed a hook "sits upstream of the grammar gates, which is precisely the
position from which a doctrine violation could land while the gates report
green." That is false: `check-grammar` and `check-cinematic-grammar` read files
as they are on disk at `prebuild`, `verify` and in CI, so a hook cannot make
them report green on a violation they cover.

The narrower objection survives, and is the real one: **the gates check
vocabulary and grammar — not copy, spacing, or token values.** They enforce D3,
D9, D1 and D2 plus cinematic purity. They say nothing about whether a line of
editorial copy was rewritten or a measure widened. That is exactly the surface a
generic polish tool acts on, and on the cinematic surface the copy and the held
timing *are* the material. So the risk is not that hooks defeat enforcement; it
is that hooks act precisely where enforcement does not look — real, reversible
(every edit is in git), and therefore a reason to **scope** rather than refuse.

The retraction is preserved rather than deleted, because a governance argument
that overstates its mechanism is weaker than the accurate one that displaces it.

The 153-file vendored payload is gitignored — `.agents/` is the institutional
memory record and a future session reading that tree must find only our own.
`skills-lock.json` is committed as the provenance receipt naming which version
touched the repo. **Ignore the payload, keep the receipt.**

### 6 · The screen record finally has a consumer (`e5af453`)

Ten species dossiers carry a real `tmdb_media` array. Until this arc the only
consumer counted it and discarded the count — film data fetched, counted, thrown
away. An eco-**cinema** observatory whose screen record was never once shown.

It now renders in the atlas field record, in the **research** register: plain
rows — year, title, classification — ordered by year. No posters, no cards, no
ratings, no marketing copy. The note above the table says what the rows are and
are not: **depictions**, a record of how the animal has been filmed; not
evidence about the animal, and nothing there sources the interaction web above
it.

Citation-or-skip holds — a place with no dossier, or an empty `tmdb_media`,
renders nothing at all, not a "no films found" placeholder. Which dossier
carries a place's screen record is a local map in the `PLACE_META` idiom, **not**
a new Place Manifest field: the manifest is governed by ADR-001, and a new
first-class term there is an architecture decision, not an implementation
detail.

Deliberately **not** reinstated on the cinematic surface: a count there would be
a dashboard-register affordance of the kind the 2026-05-24 audit removed.

### 9 · The composition scale — wiring attempted, and stopped

`src/style.css` gained a type scale, spacing rhythm and measure. The audit had
left the file a palette and two type sizes; two sizes cannot express hierarchy,
so every screen rendered in one flat register. **The tokens landed with zero
consumers**, and the commit says so plainly rather than glossing it.

This session set out to wire them and **stopped without wiring them.** Five
findings, each checked against the tree, and together they say the scale
describes a screen this repository does not have:

1. **Scope.** `src/style.css` is imported by `src/main.js` alone. The three
   place stylesheets and `field-record.css` each import only their own CSS and
   never load it, so every token resolves on the **homepage only**. A
   `var(--type-line)` written into `crossing.css` resolves to nothing and the
   declaration is dropped. `field-record.css` already shows this was hit and
   worked around during authoring: its V1.4 rules carry literal values
   hand-annotated with the token name they match.

2. **The call sites do not exist.** `--type-place` is specified for a place name
   and `--type-lead` for a standfirst. `places/crossing.html` and
   `places/epr-vents.html` contain exactly four elements between them:
   `.captions`, `.begin`, `.scroll-track`, `.noscript`. There is no title
   element and no sub-line on any cinematic place.

3. **The canonical form contradicts the composition.** The specified block is
   `position:absolute; left:var(--sp-6); bottom:var(--sp-6); text-align:left`.
   The places are `position:fixed`, centred, with all beats stacked in one grid
   cell (`grid-area: 1/1`) — the mechanism by which one beat shows at a time.
   The framing beat is centred by doctrine (Article I, Article XVII, the held
   first beat). The homepage is composed bottom-**right** on purpose: "subject
   one corner, masthead the other." Applying the form would re-compose all
   three surfaces, which this session's own instruction freezes ("every beat",
   "every homepage interaction and hit target").

4. **`rem` measure is the weaker mechanism for this type.** The captions are
   clamp-sized, so their font-size varies with the viewport. A `max-width` in
   `ch` holds the measure at a constant number of characters as the type grows;
   a `max-width: 34rem` does not — it yields more characters per line at the
   small end of the clamp and fewer at the large end. The incumbent `30ch`,
   `22ch`, `36ch`, `18ch`, `16ch` are not un-tokenized sloppiness; for
   clamp-sized type they are more correct than `--measure`. `--measure-body`
   suits the research surface, whose body type is fixed-size — and there
   `42rem` is already one of four measures in use (48/44/42/36rem).

5. **The governing rule is factually wrong.** The block claimed "300 stays the
   only weight on both surfaces." Measured: `src/style.css` is the only 300 in
   the tree. `crossing.css`, `epr-vents.css` and `sundarbans.css` all set the
   caption to **400**; `field-record.css` runs **400/500/600**. The claim
   described an intended system, not the incumbent one. It is retracted in
   place, with the measurement, rather than deleted.

Two edits were made and no pixel changed: the false weight claim is corrected,
and a scope warning now sits above the tokens so the next session does not
repeat the attempt. The tokens stay declared and unconsumed — **honestly
labelled as such** — because the alternative was to invent a composition, and
the standing instruction on conflict is to report it, not to invent.

The editorial failure is upstream and worth keeping verbatim: **a specification
that supplies values without naming their call sites produces dead
declarations; one that also assumes a composition produces declarations that
cannot be revived without a design decision.**

**RULED the same day, and applied (backlog item 10, RESOLVED).** The Chief
Architect took option 3 — scope the scale, tokenize what is already right —
and retracted the scale as authored rather than deferring it. `src/style.css`
is retitled *homepage composition tokens* with the cross-surface claim
withdrawn; `--measure` and `--measure-body` are **deleted** rather than
re-expressed in `ch`, because the only text on that surface is three
`white-space:nowrap` caption links that never wrap and so cannot take a measure
at all. The homepage took **no** type token — it has no editorial line and no
sub-line, and its three captions were already tokenized before the V1.4 block
existed — so the remaining type tokens stay declared, unconsumed and honestly
labelled rather than given a forced consumer. Each surface now names its own
measures in its own sheet, with no cross-file import: `--cap-measure` /
`--framing-measure` on both canvas places, `--question-measure` /
`--stance-measure` / `--inscription-measure` on Sundarbans, and the `--fr-*`
measure and rhythm set on the field record, which turns V1.4's hand-annotated
literals into real declarations. `ch` on the cinematic surfaces and `rem` on
the research surface are each correct for their own type sizing, and that
asymmetry is now stated in both files rather than left to be rediscovered.
**Zero pixels moved**, verified by rebuilding and comparing every rule's
declaration set across all seven CSS bundles — 352 rules, all identical.

**Then CLOSED, by deleting the scale outright.** The caption-inset check
settled it: scoping was the right first move, and the homepage then failed the
same test the other surfaces had failed. `--type-place` / `--type-line` /
`--type-lead`, the `--lh-*` and `--ls-*` sets and `--sp-1..6` are **gone** —
not kept as an honestly-labelled unused block, because a declared unconsumed
token set is a standing invitation for a later session to wire it, and it
encoded a composition this repository does not have. This project's own rule is
that speculative artifacts do not ship; well-commented dead tokens are still
dead tokens. `src/style.css` retains exactly two things, and both are facts
rather than intentions: the **measured weight retraction** (this file is the
only 300 in the tree; the places run 400; field-record runs 400/500/600) and
the **standing scope-boundary prohibition** (no sheet may import it to widen
token scope). The eleven per-sheet tokens stay — they are the durable half of
this work. Zero pixels moved again, same 353-rule check.

The composition question is not left open. The incumbent compositions are
doctrinal — Article I / Article XVII for the centred framing beat, and a
deliberate "subject one corner, masthead the other" for the homepage — so
per-surface naming is the standing pattern and there is nothing to revisit.
