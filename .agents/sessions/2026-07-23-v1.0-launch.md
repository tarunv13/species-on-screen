## What launched

The three Launch-scoped product decisions from `docs/PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md`, implemented against an approved V1 specification, editorially signed off, and deployed:

- **D1 — Remove "Choice of One":** the Sundarbans threshold no longer frames its single entry as a chooser. The `.lenses` group `aria-label` changed from "Choose a perspective" to "Enter through the place"; the visible stance line and the single lens are unchanged. No visitor-perceivable string (visual or screen-reader) at the threshold implies selection among alternatives.
- **D2 — Continuous World:** the ambient breath now continues through commitment. The rafLoop gate was extended so the threshold breath also applies while `descentState === 'descending' && descentP === 0` (committed but not yet advancing); once the descent begins moving, a `breathContinuity` multiplier ramps the breath to 0 over `BREATH_HANDOFF_MS` (900ms) so the timeline owns transforms with no snap. Breath touches parallax x/y offsets only — never `descentP`, `descentIntent`, or the descent timeline. The WP8 Amendment 4 governor is untouched; reduced-motion reuses the existing breath magnitude (no reduce branch in the breath). Continuity only — magnitude, period, and character unchanged.
- **D3 — Truthful Scope:** the homepage public description (meta / og / twitter) changed from "one inhabited place" to "three inhabited places" — the count is now accurate (Sundarbans, Crossing, East Pacific Rise).
