## What was produced

`public/dwca/epr-vents/` — five files on the Coral Triangle schema:
`occurrence.txt` (9), `resource-relationship.txt` (10), `meta.xml`, `eml.xml`,
`CREDITS.md`. The TSV files were emitted by a throwaway Node generator (kept in
scratchpad, not committed) so tab columns and JSON cells are exact.

### Occurrence model (9 taxa)

Two chemoautotroph producers (*Candidatus* Endoriftia persephone endosymbiont;
free-living *Beggiatoa* mat) → the focal giant tubeworm *Riftia pachyptila* and
the vent mussel *Bathymodiolus thermophilus* (both symbiotic consumers) → two
microbial-mat grazers (*Alvinella pompejana*, *Lepetodrilus elevatus*) → the
vent crab *Bythograea thermydron* (predator) → the vent eelpout *Thermarces
cerberus* (apex) → *Homo sapiens* (research + prospective mining). `trophicRole`
/ `cascadeFailOrder` reuse the vocabulary already in `places.config.json`.

### Interaction model (10, OBO RO typed)

`hasHost` (RO_0002454) for the chemosynthetic endosymbiosis; `eats`
(RO_0002470) for mat grazing; `preysOn` (RO_0002439) for the crab/fish
predation chain; `interactsWith` (RO_0002437) for the two human relationships
(observation; mining/ISA). All nine actors participate; referential integrity
of every `resourceID`/`relatedResourceID` was checked programmatically.

## The three challenges the M15 ruling flagged — resolved

1. **Jurisdiction (international waters).** The 9–10°N EPR segment is in areas
   beyond national jurisdiction (high seas; seabed = "the Area" under UNCLOS
   Part XI / ISA). `countryCode` empty; ABNJ/ISA stated in `eml.xml` and the
   human mining interaction (REL:10).
2. **Observational access (2,500 m).** `basisOfRecord = HumanObservation` for
   the metazoa and human (documented by DSV *Alvin* / ROV); `MaterialSample`
   for the two microbial taxa (identified from collected samples). REL:9 makes
   the submersible-observation relationship itself explicit.
3. **Vent chemistry as abiotic actor.** Darwin Core Occurrence is a *taxon*
   standard, so the abiotic energy base is **not** a fake taxon row; it is
   `habitat` + `dynamicProperties.energySource` on the two chemoautotrophs +
   relationship remarks. Taxonomic validity preserved.

The M15 amendment path (fall back to Amazon várzea) was **not** triggered.

## Taxonomic validation

Every taxon verified live against the GBIF species-match API (2026-07-02).
Eight species keys returned EXACT / ACCEPTED / confidence 99. Discipline
points, all following the Coral Triangle M9C precedent (match the backbone;
never fabricate):
- *Candidatus* Endoriftia persephone is not in the backbone — the record keeps
  the correct species name and anchors `gbifTaxonKey` to the accepted **genus**
  Endoriftia (10685420), with the note in `associatedReferences`.
- *Alvinella pompejana*: backbone assigns no `order` → left empty.
- *Thermarces cerberus*: backbone assigns no `class` → left empty (Zoarcidae in
  Perciformes).
