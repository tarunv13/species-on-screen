# Observatory sound — design note

*Steward-approved design note (V1.1 Editorial Content Pass, W4). Prose class
(`docs/`). This note records the approved sound philosophy; it does not
unreserve Article XVI (audio remains reserved territory per
`private-book/continuity/continuity-dossier.md` §4) and authorizes no new
implementation.*

The Observatory's sound is **breath-rate ambience on incommensurable
periods** — never melodic, never a cue, never an event. Magnitude may scale;
character may not.

What this means in practice:

- **Breath-rate.** The register of slow respiration, not rhythm. Periods sit
  in the tens of seconds, below musical perception.
- **Incommensurable periods.** Component cycles never align to a perceivable
  repeat — the same construction the visual breath already uses
  (prime-length periods that never phase-lock). The ambience never "loops"
  to the ear.
- **Never melodic.** No pitch sequence, no interval structure, no motif.
- **Never a cue.** Sound never signals an interactive opportunity, a state
  change, or a reward. It carries no information the visitor is meant to act
  on.
- **Never an event.** Nothing punches in, stings, or resolves. Sound widens
  and settles; it does not happen *at* the visitor.
- **Magnitude may scale; character may not.** Loudness and density may vary
  with context (entry ramps, reduced-motion or quiet contexts, per-place
  atmosphere), but the ambience's nature — breath-rate, non-melodic,
  non-signalling — is invariant across every place and every state.

The shipped Sundarbans ambient bed (detuned low sines under a slow LFO,
filtered noise beds, gesture-unlocked, ~5s widening ramp) is the existing
instance of this philosophy, and any future sound work is bound by this note
plus whatever ruling unreserves Article XVI.
