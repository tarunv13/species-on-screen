### TSB-4 — Coroller (2023)

**paper_id:** Coroller2023
**First author:** Coroller
**Year:** 2023
**Title:** Video games as a tool for ecological learning: the case of Animal Crossing
**Venue:** Ecosphere

**Why this paper is difficult:**
Two independent rule failures are likely: (1) The KF-D1/D2 distinction requires a trigger-word check (HB-R4) — the journal venue (Ecosphere is the journal of the Ecological Society of America) should activate the check even though the title alone doesn't confirm participant data. (2) The NC-2/NC-3 boundary is live: "ecological learning" could mean individual student outcomes (NC-2) or conservation-sector literacy outcomes (NC-3). Do not assign NC until you have determined KF, because the NC framing depends on who the learning claims are directed at.

**What to watch for:** Run HB-R4 before committing KF. For ER, traverse the tree for Animal Crossing specifically — it is well documented; many real species (fish, insects, fossils) are named in the game by real names. Determine whether any specific real-world location is named in the game, not in the paper's conservation discussion.

### TSB-5 — Dorward (2016)

**paper_id:** Dorward2016
**First author:** Dorward
**Year:** 2016
**Title:** Pokémon Go: Benefits, Costs, and Lessons for the Conservation Movement
**Venue:** Conservation Letters

**Why this paper is difficult:**
Conservation Letters is a high-impact conservation science journal. The coder's first instinct will be KF-D2 (empirical conservation study). But "Benefits, Costs, and Lessons" is analytical/argumentative framing, not empirical framing. Run HB-R4 before deciding. The paper was published in 2016, the year Pokémon Go launched — assess whether a rigorous empirical participant study was feasible by publication date. For NC, the phrase "for the Conservation Movement" is precise: who is the audience? What level of social organization does that represent?

**What to watch for:** Distinguish analytical/review papers from empirical papers (HB-R4). "Lessons for X" directed at a sector or movement = specific NC level. Do not confuse the journal's typical content with this paper's specific method.

**paper_id:** Dunn2021
**First author:** Dunn
**Year:** 2021
**Title:** Stepping into the Wildeverse: Evaluating the impact of augmented reality mobile gaming on pro-conservation behaviours
**Venue:** People and Nature

**Why this paper is difficult:**
Before entering any decision tree, answer the scope question in §1.2: is the Wildeverse a digital game? The title says "mobile gaming." If you are uncertain, assign `in_scope = Uncertain` and document your reasoning in coder_notes before proceeding. Assuming you determine it is in scope: the ER path requires understanding what the Wildeverse shows users — are real species shown in a real location, or in the user's own environment (AR overlay with no fixed ecosystem location)? **First author:** Abraham

### TSB-10 — Hobbs (2019)

**paper_id:** Hobbs2019a
**First author:** Hobbs
**Year:** 2019
**Title:** Science Hunters: Teaching Science Concepts in Schools Using Minecraft
**Venue:** ARISE – The Journal of Action Research in Education

**Why this paper is difficult:**
"Science Hunters" may appear to be the name of the game, but it is not — identify the actual game in the title. Once you have the coding unit: ER is straightforward for this game, but requires thinking about what the paper's research question asks the game to do. For KF, this paper sits at the KF-B/KF-D2 boundary: is the governing question about what science is in the game (KF-B) or about whether the game produces learning outcomes (KF-D2)? Apply HB-R4 and consider what type of research an action research journal publishes. For NC, the phrase "Teaching Science Concepts in Schools" has a direct implication — what level of claim does learning in a school context represent?

**What to watch for:** Identify the game first. Apply HB-R4. Distinguish KF-B (science content analysis) from KF-D2 (empirical learning study). The journal's methodological tradition is a strong signal.
