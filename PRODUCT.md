## What it is

An eco-cinema observatory that carries **one attested ecological record
honestly across three surfaces** — cinematic, research, atlas.
*(Source: concept note; Observatory Narrative Stack.)*

## Positioning / thesis

A stranger is lowered into a living place until they care, and that care ends
in **understanding rather than a completion state**. Restraint is the material
of trust.
*(Source: Knowledge Experience Philosophy.)*

## Platform

`web`. Static site, Vite-built, deployed to GitHub Pages.

## Stack

Vanilla ES modules + Vite. Three.js (planetary and place rendering), GSAP
(timeline), Lenis (scroll). No framework, no CSS framework, no runtime UI
library. Darwin Core Archives (DwC-A + EML) as the data substrate.
*(Source: `package.json`; verified against the tree, not assumed.)*

## Success

**Understanding, unmeasured.**

The project deliberately has **no success metric**. There are no analytics, no
completion state, and no progress indicator, because a metric would convert
understanding into a score and the score would become the thing optimised.
*(Source: Canon XIV.)*

## Evidence posture

Reach, not truth. The evidential surface reports how far a warrant *reaches*
(`REACHES EVIDENCE` / `REACHES A SOURCE` / `REACH INCOMPLETE`) and never
pass/fail. A `confidence` field is permanently rejected.
*(Source: M33 (D7), M34 (D6); Thought System ADR.)*

Unresolved identifiers stay unresolved. Bates et al. 2005 is the standing
precedent: two milestones of work concluded with an explicit non-resolution
rather than a guessed DOI.
*(Source: M31, M32.)*
