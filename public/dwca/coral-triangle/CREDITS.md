## Taxonomic notes

- *Chlorurus microrhinos*: GBIF backbone (key 5211130) retains Order Perciformes / Family Scaridae as of 2026-06-27. Multiple checklists (CoL, FishBase) assign to Order Labriformes / Family Labridae following Near et al. (2012). DwC-A uses backbone values; backbone revision expected.
- *Acanthaster planci*: GBIF backbone retains *A. planci* (key 2271208) for the Coral Triangle. Kamya et al. (2018) distinguish *A. cf. solaris* for the Pacific clade; backbone not yet updated.
- *Lobophora variegata*: GBIF backbone key 3200482 confirmed, Kingdom Chromista / Phylum Ochrophyta. Design spec v1 error (Kingdom Plantae) corrected in v2.
